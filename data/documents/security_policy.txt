Nimbus Labs Security Policy

Passwords and authentication. All employees must use the company password manager and enable two-factor authentication on every work account. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Shared passwords must never be sent by email or chat.

Data encryption. Customer files stored in Nimbus Cloud Storage are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Encryption keys are rotated every 90 days.

Incident reporting. Any suspected security incident must be reported to security@nimbuslabs.example within one hour of discovery. The security team will acknowledge the report within 30 minutes during business hours.

Laptops. Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and automatically lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately so they can be remotely wiped.
