# Nimbus Labs Employee Handbook

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year, and they expire on 31 March. Leave requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.

## Remote work
Nimbus Labs follows a hybrid model. Employees work from the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays and may work remotely on the other days. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from the department head.

## Learning budget
Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 USD. It can be used for courses, books, and conferences. Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of purchase together with the receipt.
