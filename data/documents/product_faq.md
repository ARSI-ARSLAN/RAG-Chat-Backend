# Nimbus Cloud Storage - Product FAQ

## Plans and pricing
Nimbus offers three plans. The Free plan includes 5 GB of storage. The Pro plan costs 9 USD per month and includes 500 GB of storage. The Team plan costs 15 USD per user per month and includes 2 TB of shared storage plus an admin console.

## File size limits
The maximum size of a single uploaded file is 2 GB on the Free plan and 50 GB on Pro and Team plans.

## Version history
Nimbus keeps previous versions of every file for 30 days on the Pro plan and 180 days on the Team plan. The Free plan does not include version history.

## Support
Support is available by email at all times. Live chat support is available on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00 UTC and is limited to Pro and Team customers.
